Privacy Policy

NEO Media Productions Inc · Last updated 14 August 2026

This policy explains how NEO Media Productions Inc (“we,” “us”) handles information in connection with this website, neomediainc.com. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information we collect

This website is a static brochure site. It has no accounts, no login, no comment forms, and no advertising or tracking cookies. We do not run analytics that profile you.

  • If you email us — using the address on our site — we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we keep that correspondence so we can reply and provide the service you asked about.
  • Server logs — our web host may record standard technical request data (such as IP address, browser type, and time of request) for security and reliability. We do not use it to identify you.
  • Web fonts — our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those files from Google, which means Google may receive your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy for details.

How we use information

Only to respond to enquiries, to provide photo-editing services you request, and to keep the site secure and working. We do not sell or rent your information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that operate our website and email (for example, our hosting and email providers), and only as needed to run those services — or where required by law.

Retention

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to handle your request and our ordinary business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what correspondence we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we’ll take care of it.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

The FlatOut app

Our motorsport app, FlatOut, has its own privacy policy that applies inside the app. See flatoutapp.app/privacy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and post the new version here.
