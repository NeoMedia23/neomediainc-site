NEO Media Productions is an automotive photography and software studio in Meridian, Idaho. We shoot vehicles, grade every frame ourselves, and build the tools we couldn't find.

Services

What we do

Two disciplines, one studio. The photography pays attention to surfaces and light; the software brings the same attention to how things are built.

Vehicle photography

Full walkarounds for private sellers, dealers, and collectors — exteriors, interiors, engine bays, and detail work. Shot on location around the Treasure Valley.

Retouching & colour grade

Every frame is graded by hand. Reflection cleanup, background work, and a consistent look across a full set so a listing or a portfolio reads as one piece.

Software

Custom application development, built and maintained in-house. FlatOut is our own product; we take selective client work alongside it.

Portfolio

Selected frames

FlatOut

A motorsport companion app for fans who follow every session, not just the race.

  • Season calendar with every session, in your own time zone
  • Driver and constructor standings, updated through the season
  • Circuit maps traced in-house rather than licensed, with sector and start-finish detail
  • A private predictions league you run with friends, scored automatically once results are official
  • Home-screen widgets for the next session and current standings

Built and maintained by NEO Media Productions. Coming to iOS and Android.
