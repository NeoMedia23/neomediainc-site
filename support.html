FlatOut app

FlatOut is an independent, unofficial Formula 1 companion app, in development for iOS, with Android to follow. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Formula 1 or the FIA. If you hit a problem or have a request, email us with:

Your device and OS version
For example, “iPhone 15, iOS 18.5.” It helps us reproduce the issue.
What happened
What you did, what you expected, and what the app did instead. A screenshot is welcome.
App version
Shown on the app’s Settings screen, if you can find it.

The app’s privacy policy is at flatoutapp.app/privacy.

Photo editing

For retouching or colour-grading enquiries, email us with a sample photo and how many frames you need edited. We work with clients anywhere, and reply within two business days.
